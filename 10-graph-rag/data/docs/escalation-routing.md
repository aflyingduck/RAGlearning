# Solstice Labs — Internal Support Escalation Routing

This document describes how customer support tickets get routed between
teams. It does not duplicate the customer-facing troubleshooting or
warranty policies (see those documents) — it only describes which team
owns which situation.

**Networking issues** (Wi-Fi drops, Zigbee pairing failures, Hub Mesh not
linking) are triaged first by the Connectivity Team.

**Hardware defects.** If a Connectivity Team engineer determines during
triage that the root cause of a networking issue is a hardware fault
rather than a configuration or network problem, the ticket is escalated to
the Hardware Team, who resolve it under Support Track 3.

**Account and billing issues** (subscription charges, plan changes,
refunds unrelated to a warranty claim) are handled directly by the Billing
Team under Support Track 1.

**Privacy and data requests** (data export, deletion requests, questions
about what's stored) are routed to the Trust & Safety Team under Support
Track 4, regardless of which product feature the request concerns.

**Software and firmware issues** not caused by hardware faults (app bugs,
firmware update failures, sync problems) stay with the Connectivity Team
under Support Track 2.

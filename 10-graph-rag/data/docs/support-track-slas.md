# Solstice Labs — Support Track SLAs

Internal service-level targets by support track. Track numbers are
assigned during escalation routing (see escalation-routing.md) and are
the single source of truth for response-time commitments — customer-
facing docs do not always restate them.

- **Support Track 1**: resolved within 1 business day.
- **Support Track 2**: resolved within 3 business days.
- **Support Track 3**: resolved within 7 business days.
- **Support Track 4**: resolved within 14 calendar days, per regulatory
  requirements for data-related requests.

These are internal ticket-resolution targets, separate from (and not
always equal to) the customer-facing timelines quoted elsewhere, such as
warranty claim processing times.

Tracks are reviewed quarterly and re-numbered if team ownership changes;
always confirm current track assignments against escalation-routing.md
rather than assuming these numbers are permanent.

# Nimbus Hub — Subscription Plans

The Hub works fully offline for local automations without a subscription.
A Nimbus Plus subscription ($5/month or $48/year, updated pricing effective
Q3 2026) unlocks cloud features:

- Remote access to the Hub from outside your home network
- 30-day history of sensor and automation logs (vs. 24 hours on the free tier)
- Voice model updates that improve recognition accuracy over time
- Integration with third-party services like weather and calendar providers

Nimbus Plus does not change local automation speed or reliability — those
run entirely on-device regardless of subscription status. If a Plus
subscription lapses, the Hub keeps functioning locally; only the cloud
features above stop working.

Family plans covering up to 5 hubs at one address cost $8/month. There is no
per-device fee; pricing is per hub (or per family plan), not per connected
sensor or light.

Subscriptions can be cancelled anytime from the app under Settings >
Account > Subscription. Refunds for annual plans are prorated for unused
months if cancelled within the first 90 days.
